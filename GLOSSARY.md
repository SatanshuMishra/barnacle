# Barnacle

Barnacle is a Discord bot for a World of Warships clan: it schedules Clan Battle events, runs games, and runs the IQ Competition.

## Events

**Event**:
A recurring block of Clan Battle sessions between a first and last day, on chosen weekdays, at one start time in one time zone.

**Session**:
One occurrence of an event on one day.
_Avoid_: Match, game night

**Template**:
A preset that fills an event's weekdays, start, length and zone, such as Clan Battles.

**Panel**:
The private message of select menus and buttons where one member builds or edits an event or competition, step by step.
_Avoid_: Form, dialog

## IQ Competition

**IQ Competition**:
A points competition among the clan, with its own category, an announcements channel and a submissions channel; one per server at a time.
_Avoid_: Contest, tournament

**Criterion**:
A named feat worth a fixed number of points that a player can claim by submission.
_Avoid_: Challenge, task

**Submission**:
A message in the submissions channel naming one criterion, with exactly 2 screenshots attached.
_Avoid_: Entry, claim

**Judge**:
A member holding the IQ-Judge role, who awards points by reacting and adjusts points by command.

**Judging reaction**:
The one emoji that, added by a judge to a valid submission, awards its points.

**Target role**:
A role whose members can see the competition's channels and are pinged by its announcements.

**Competition template**:
One of the server's 10 newest competitions, whose criteria, rules, roles, judges and judging reaction a new competition can copy.

**Archive**:
The state of a cleaned-up competition: its channels are gone, its criteria, rules and scores are kept.
