# Bot name candidates

Date: 2026-09-16 (revised the same day)

**Decision (2026-09-16): Barnacle.** The GitHub repository is now `SatanshuMishra/barnacle`. The list below is kept as the record of what was considered.

## Brief

The name is a mascot: a small, cute character people remember, in the way "shrimpy" is. It must be nautical, fit a World of Warships community, and not describe any one feature, because the bot will grow. Role and object names such as Bosun or Binnacle were rejected for this reason: they describe a job, not a character.

A name scores well when it:

- is a creature or a small, friendly vessel you could draw as an avatar,
- is one or two syllables, or has an obvious short form,
- has a naval or warship link that a WoWS player might enjoy spotting.

Availability is **unchecked** for every name: Discord application names, GitHub organisations, domains, crates.io and trademarks.

## Recommended (unranked)

| Name | The mascot | Naval link | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guppy** | A tiny, round fish | GUPPY was the US Navy's post-war program to modernise its submarines - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Underwater_Propulsion_Power_Program) | Several unrelated products use the name `[unverified]` |
| **Pollywog** | A tadpole ("Wog" is a poor short form; use "Polly") | In the line-crossing ceremony, a sailor who has never crossed the equator is a pollywog and becomes a shellback afterwards - [USS Midway Museum](https://www.midway.org/blog/line-crossing-traditions). That gives the bot a built-in rank ladder for future features. | Avoid "wog" as a nickname; it is also an ethnic slur |
| **Limpet** | A small cone-shelled snail that clings to rocks | The limpet mine, a naval charge stuck to a ship's hull, is named after it - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limpet_mine) | Less instantly "cute" without a good avatar |
| **Krill** | A tiny shrimp-like crustacean | None beyond the sea; it is shrimpy's closest cousin, so the character carries over | Plain word, likely widely used |
| **Cuttle** | A cuttlefish, which changes colour to blend in | Camouflage is a large part of World of Warships, and the name sounds like "cuddle" | Less familiar word |
| **Tuggy** | A small tugboat with a face | Tugs guide big warships in and out of harbour, which is what a helper bot does | Close to existing children's-show tugboat characters `[unverified]` |
| **Nudi** | A nudibranch, a brightly coloured sea slug | None; chosen for colour, which suits a bot with its own palette | Needs a line of explanation |
| **Barnacle** | A barnacle stuck to a hull | Every warship collects them; reads as "here for the long haul" | Three syllables; "Barney" as a short form collides with a well-known character |

## Also considered

| Name | Note |
|---|---|
| Scampi | Keeps the shrimp character; also a dish name |
| Sprat | A tiny fish; short and punchy |
| Puffin | A seabird; "Puffin" is also a well-known book imprint `[unverified]` |
| Squib | A little squid; in gunnery a squib is a round that fires weakly, a fun in-joke |
| Periwinkle ("Winkle") | A sea snail; also a colour name |
| Otto | A sea otter; a name that feels like a character |
| Dinghy | The smallest boat aboard; cute to say |
| Salty | A salty little crab; in gamer slang "salty" means sore after losing, which a WoWS community will recognise |

## Names to avoid

Nemo, Dumbo (octopus), Pingu, Plankton, Skipper, Inky and Bubbles are already well-known characters owned by others.

## About "shrimpy" itself

Shrimpy was a crypto trading-bot platform, which announced it was ending the business with a last day of operations on 7 July 2023 - [Brave New Coin](https://bravenewcoin.com/insights/crypto-trading-bots-in-trouble). Later reports of an acquisition are `[unverified]`. The name is still usable for a Discord bot, but search results for "Shrimpy bot" will point at that product for some time.
