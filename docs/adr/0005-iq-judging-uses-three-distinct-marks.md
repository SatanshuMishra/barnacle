# IQ judging uses three distinct marks, and a well-formed post carries no mark until awarded

Each competition has a Denied, an Awarded and a Confirm awarded emoji, any standard or custom emoji and never two the same. The bot puts Denied on a malformed submission, leaves a well-formed one unmarked until a judge reacts with Awarded, then adds Confirm awarded, so a post's marks alone say whether it needs fixing, is waiting, or has its points. Marking every well-formed post with a check on arrival was rejected because the same check could not then mean that points were given. Existing competitions keep their emoji as Awarded; rolling the change back turns a custom Awarded mark into the trophy.
