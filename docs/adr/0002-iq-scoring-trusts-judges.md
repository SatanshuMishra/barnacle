# IQ scoring trusts judges and checks only a submission's format

The IQ Competition is played among friends, so the bot checks only a submission's format (the whole text names a criterion ignoring capitals, and exactly 2 images are attached) and never its content. Points come only from a judge's judging reaction on a valid post, once per post, or from a judge's /iq points command; judges may approve their own posts, and removing a reaction never takes points back. A policed design, with reaction removal revoking points or a second judge confirming, was rejected as friction a friendly competition does not need.
